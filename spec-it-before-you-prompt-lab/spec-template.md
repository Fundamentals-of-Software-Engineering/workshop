# Spec: (feature name)

Keep it to one page. Bullets are fine. If a section doesn't apply, write "none" so the reader knows
you thought about it.

## Goal

What problem does this solve, and for whom? One or two sentences. Say why, not just what.

-

## Users

Who uses this? Where in the app do they meet it?

-

## Behavior

What happens, from the user's side. Use real screens, fields and examples.

-

## Edge cases

What could go wrong or be surprising? Always think about:

- Data that already exists
- Empty, invalid, huge, tiny and weird input
- Editing, not just creating
- Other languages, locales and units
- Two people at once

-
